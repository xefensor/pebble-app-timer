Timer 1.0.2

First stable preparation for Pebble Time 2 and Round 2.

Save up to eight timers and create, pause, resume, reset and reuse them with the watch buttons. A finished timer can be snoozed for one minute or dismissed.

Time 2 adds an optional audible alarm alongside vibration. Sound defaults on and respects system mute and Quiet Time; Round 2 uses vibration. The sound preference is independent of appearance settings.

A countdown can appear as an Activity on Dynamic, Halftone or Dashboard. This optional sharing needs Pebble plugin support and a connected phone. Timer continues to own the countdown and alarm on the watch.
