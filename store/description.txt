Up to eight timers, directly on Pebble.

Activities are live updates shown by compatible XOCE apps and watchfaces, such as a countdown, music or a new notification.

Install Dynamic or Halftone and choose it as your watchface. Enable activity plugins and show Timer in that watchface’s activity list, then save. Start a countdown in Timer and return to the clock to see the time remaining. Timer still runs the countdown and alarm. Sharing is optional; Timer works on its own.

Sharing activities between apps needs plugin support in the Pebble phone app, the required permissions and a connected phone.

Create a timer with separate hours, minutes and seconds. Pause, resume, reset or delete it from the watch. The list keeps countdown and state together when they fit; the detail screen keeps its large countdown. Timers survive closing and reopening the app.

Native alarms work without a connected phone. Snooze for one minute or finish the alarm from the watch. Optional Pebble activity publishing makes your timer available to compatible faces and apps.

Choose Small, Medium, Large or Follow Pebble text and your display appearance. Available in 17 languages. No account, advertisements or analytics.
